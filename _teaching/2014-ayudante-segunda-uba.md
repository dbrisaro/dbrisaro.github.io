**Position:** Teaching Assistant (2014–2017)  
**Institution:** University of Buenos Aires, Department of Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences

**Courses taught:**
* **Atmospheric and Oceanic Dynamics** (2015, 2016–2017)
* **General Circulation of the Ocean and Atmosphere** (2017)
* **Theoretical Oceanography** (2015, 2016)
* **Introduction to Coastal Engineering** (2014)

This position involved supporting course instruction, assisting students with exercises, and gaining experience in academic teaching within the atmospheric and oceanic sciences department. Responsibilities included helping students with problem sets, laboratory work, and providing additional support during office hours.
