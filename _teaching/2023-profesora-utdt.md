**Position:** Guest Professor (2023–Present)  
**Course:** Digitalization and Data Use in Government  
**Institution:** Universidad Torcuato Di Tella, School of Government

This graduate-level course focuses on the digitalization and use of data in government, covering topics related to public policy analysis, data-driven decision making, and digital transformation in the public sector. The course examines how governments can leverage technology and data analytics to improve policy effectiveness and public service delivery.
