**Position:** Head Teaching Assistant (2018–Present)  
**Institution:** University of Buenos Aires, Department of Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences

**Courses taught:**
* **General Oceanography** (First semester, 2022–present)
* **Atmospheric and Oceanic Dynamics** (First semester, until 2022)
* **General Circulation of the Ocean and Atmosphere** (Second semester, until 2022)

This teaching position involves leading practical laboratory sessions, supervising undergraduate students, and supporting theoretical instruction with hands-on oceanographic and atmospheric science exercises. Responsibilities include designing laboratory experiments, grading assignments, and mentoring students in their research projects.
