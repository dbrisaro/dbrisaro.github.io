**Position:** Teaching Assistant (2022)  
**Course:** Public Policy Design  
**Institution:** Universidad Torcuato Di Tella, School of Government

This graduate course focuses on public policy design, covering methodological approaches, analytical frameworks, and practical tools for developing effective public policies. The course provides students with essential skills for policy analysis, design thinking approaches, and implementation strategies in public administration.
